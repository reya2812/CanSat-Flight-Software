# Requirements Traceability

**Created:** 2026-11-18 (Phase 1). **Update this single table after each Phase 2 module.**

**How to read the labels:**
- The "PDR wording" column is quoted **verbatim** from `references/CanSat_PDR.pdf`, so every cell in it is **[PDR]**.
- The "Software module" and "Planned test case" columns are **[RECOMMENDATION]** (proposed in `PHASE1_PROPOSAL.md` §4 and §6).
- The "Status" column holds the current state. Every row starts as **not started**.
- "Related project ID" refers to `docs/02_system_requirements.md` (SW-xx) or `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` (OD-xxx). Those are project-document IDs, not PDR IDs.

**ID rules:**
- PDR IDs are kept exactly as written: SR-1…SR-7 (slide 6) and compliance #1…#6 (slide 21).
- PDR statements that have no ID are shown with "(no ID)" plus their slide number. **No requirement IDs have been invented.**
- Test-case IDs (TC-…) are planned software test identifiers. They are not requirement IDs.
- Hardware tests (HW: …) are named after the slide 20 test rows.

**About the two lists:** they do not match one-to-one. The differences are recorded in OD-033 and OD-034.
